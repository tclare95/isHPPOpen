These are current-code findings, risks and investigation options, **not prescribed implementation details or deployment approval**. Product invariants are in [the capability spec](specs/predictor-observation-source/spec.md).

## Contract fit and unresolved questions

1. **Identity and values:** RDP level data use EA Flood Monitoring `mASD`/ASD; the predictor's fallback/history uses Hydrology `...-level-i-900-m-qualified`. Prove equivalence, including datum and level precision, before mapping. Rainfall uses observed `...-rainfall-t-900-mm-qualified` totals, not level/flow measurements. #52 six-gauge dashboard parity does **not** deliver predictor rainfall coverage.
2. **History shape:** a normal 10-day predictor context is within RDP's 366-day history query bound and typically manageable with the existing per-measure endpoint. Confirm actual volume, pagination, latency, gaps, 15-minute alignment, source duplicates, fill behaviour and the impact of unknown coverage; do not demand a new batch-history route without evidence.
3. **Freshness:** the 15-minute RDP collector introduces collection/persistence/read timing beyond EA publication. RDP `fresh` currently means observation age no more than two 15-minute intervals, not that RDP matches the newest direct EA sample. Compare `observedAt`, RDP `ingestedAt`, health `lastSourceObservedAt`, direct-EA latest time and actual request/forecast timestamps. Distinguish time-since-measurement from source and pipeline lag; measure normal and high-water runs with failures and late corrections. The model's `forecast_time` is the latest observation anchor, not necessarily the execution/issue time.
4. **Correction/replay:** current history exposes the **winning revision** plus `ingestedAt` and source reference, not a general historical `asOf=<forecast-issued-at>` query. `requestAsOf` pins the read request, not historical availability. A historical replay therefore cannot assume today's corrected history was available then. Determine whether recorded forecast-run inputs/availability evidence or a minimal separate as-known-at facility is actually necessary, without forcing a bitemporal data platform.
5. **Gap/fallback semantics:** current predictor `ffill().bfill()` can hide source gaps and backfill values before they were known. A future adapter should preserve existing model compatibility without silently masking degraded data; choose explicit eligible feature windows/availability and establish parity before tightening fill policy. Never write direct fallback samples into RDP automatically from the predictor.
6. **Authorisation and geography:** test eu-west-2 Lambda-to-eu-west-1 IAM-signed read latency, cross-region errors and limited-permission access separately. Existing Vercel-specific caller trust cannot simply be reused; production and staging role separation remains intact. Do not move compute regions solely for this feature.

## Candidate source policy (for evidence-based approval)

**RDP-first is a candidate, not an unconditional decision.** Prefer canonical verified observations when identity, coverage and age are eligible. A direct-EA check/fallback may be needed when the RDP read fails, a required series is missing, or RDP is behind live EA by more than the empirically approved prediction tolerance—even if RDP labels its last row `fresh`. Preserve EA Flood Monitoring priority for compatible live levels and Hydrology as its slower fallback/observed rain source; weather forecast input stays independent.

Source selection should be transparent per logical measurement and forecast run, including which observation timestamps were actually used; avoid mixing incompatible datums or assuming that successful HTTP responses imply contemporaneous features. At decision time, disallow readings dated after the feature/forecast anchor. Do not invent synthetic samples to make source comparison look complete. A conditional choice can be **retain direct EA as primary** if high-water evidence shows unacceptable RDP lag.

## Candidate follow-on implementation slices (not authorised here)

These are **options for later issues/PRs**, not compulsory layers or a mandated PR count:

1. **Prerequisites in RDP:** only the missing, justified measurement identities and collector/provider capability (notably rainfall) plus any narrow read/caller authorisation improvements; validate source and datum parity before expanding production collection.
2. **Predictor read-only shadow:** obtain existing RDP history/latest and derive the current model's inputs alongside the unchanged direct-EA production run. Compare observation ages, feature rows, runtime/price and failure cases without writing forecasts or changing the authoritative source.
3. **Conditional fallback and controlled activation:** after a measured incident-safe acceptance decision, permit explicit RDP-first selection with direct-EA fallback, source/age diagnostics, bounded errors and a separately reviewed switch/rollback. Keep the deployed model, output keys and hourly schedule stable.
4. **Later forecast publication/query (independent):** only if justified by a consumer, implement the neutral RDP forecast boundary from #90 and migrate readers separately; never make this a prerequisite for the predictor observation switch.

## Distinct future forecast outputs boundary

[Forecast publication #90](../forecast-publication-contract/proposal.md) already describes model-agnostic run semantics and legacy/public compatibility. A separately approved **future** RDP publication/query capability may be the home for independently authored forecast runs (government or ML), with producer/run identity, issued-at, observation/source context, target-time series, units/datum, model/version when applicable, uncertainty and revision/freshness. It should use a forecast-appropriate contract and retention, **not** persist forecasts as scalar observed samples. `trent-predictor` remains the Python training/inference/scheduling/model-release owner and continues writing current CSV keys until an independent publish and consumer cutover. Neither this investigation nor #90 creates a live forecast API.
